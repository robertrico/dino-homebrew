# The FPGA twin

DINO's third twin (Rico, 2026-10-30), after the breadboard and `netsim/`: the
Rev 1.0 mainboard and its cards as synthesizable Verilog, generated from the
same KiCad netlists. It runs in Verilator and on the Lattice ECP5-5G Versa,
at 1.0 MHz CLK (48 MHz `clk_sys`). It proves the drawing's logic, not its
timing.

## The slot area

The mainboard's eight sockets J1-J8 are eight copies of one card API. A card
plugs into any slot; the slot is a position, not an address: the card's own
'138 on M11-M13 picks its 2K window in 0x4000-0x7FFF.

    docs/notes/fpga_slots_gen.py   SLOTS: the one slot table
        J1  dino_io         card 0, DIP switch   0x4000   (KiCad)
        J2  dino_serial     card 1, 16550        0x4800   (KiCad)
        J3  deadbeef_card   card 2, DE AD BE EF  0x5000   (RTL)

## The card API

Every card, generated from KiCad or written by hand, is a module whose port
list is `gen/dino_card_ports.vh`: `clk`, `live`, `contention`, and for each of
the 37 non-rail pins of the 50-pin edge (`bus_gen.BUS`), named `e<pin>_<signal>`:

    input  eP_S_xo, eP_S_xe    what the rest of the machine drives on the pin
    output eP_S_do, eP_S_de    what the card drives, and whether

An undriven pin reads 1 (`gen/dino_card_in.vh` gives each pin as `in_<signal>`).
A pin the card does not take drives `_de = 0`. No inout. Two drivers on one
net raise `contention`. A card with off-edge I/O (switches, a serial line)
has extra ports named `io_*`, and its registry entry binds them to
`dino_system`'s own ports. Full statement: the `fpga_twin_gen.py` docstring.

## Two kinds of card, one API

The API is the port list, not the file's shape. A card is either drawn or
written, and the two look nothing alike inside:

    kind   source                         module                 shape inside
    kicad  <card>/<card>.kicad_sch        gen/<card>.v           netlist dump
    rtl    cards/<name>.v                 cards/<name>.v         behavioural

**KiCad cards** (`dino_io`, `dino_serial`) are generated by
`docs/notes/fpga_card_gen.py` from the card's netlist and nothing else, into
`gen/` (not versioned; `make gen` rebuilds it). The module is the netlist: a
`wire n_*` per KiCad net (unconnected pads included), one instance per chip
wired by pin number, the contract ports written out inline in name order, an
`xe ? xo : 1` read per taken pin, `_de = 0` on every pin not taken, and
`contention` on every net with two possible drivers: card zero's W bits
(the '244's enables against the bus's `_xe`), the serial card's W and SD
bits ('245 and 16550) and `~{IRQ}` (the 7406 against the bus). The edge pins come from the card's `DINO_BUS_EDGE` symbol (card
zero's J1, the serial card's J2); a card drawn without one takes its labels,
a label named after a bus signal on that signal's `bus_gen.BUS` pin. Do not
edit `gen/`; edit the drawing.

    card         chips                               off-edge ports       bound to
    dino_io      U76 '138, SWITCH_GATE1 '244         io_SW0..7_xo/_xe     sw_closed
    dino_serial  U101 '138, U102 '245, U103 16550,   io_SIN_xo/_xe,       sin, sout
                 U104 7406                           io_SOUT

**RTL cards** (`deadbeef_card`, `proto_card`) are hand-written. They include
`gen/dino_card_ports.vh` as their port list and `gen/dino_card_in.vh` for
`in_<signal>`, and read in three sections: the slot decode
(`cards/card_window.v`), the card (`rdata` from `offset`; take `in_W*` on
`win_wr`), the edge (W driven on `win_rd`, every other pin `_de = 0`). An RTL
card gets its window as the `WINDOW` parameter from its registry entry.

**The decode is not the same on every card.** `card_window.v` decodes as the
serial card does: '138 on M11-M13 enabled by `~{IO_SEL}`, W driven only on
`~{IO_RD_Q}`, taken on `~{IO_WR}` (PHASE_G's rule). Card zero, the older
read-only card, enables its '138 on `~{IO_RD_Q}` and leaves `~{IO_SEL}` and
`~{IO_WR}` unconnected.

## Prototyping a card

1. Copy `cards/proto_card.v` to `cards/<name>.v` and rename the module. It
   already decodes its window as the serial card does (`cards/card_window.v`)
   and drives nothing else. Fill in "the card".
2. Add its entry to `docs/notes/fpga_cards.py` `CARDS`: `kind: 'rtl'`, `src`,
   `window`, and its oracle model (`read(offset, ctx) -> byte`; `None` if it
   cannot be modelled, as the serial card's UART).
3. Seat it: one line in `fpga_slots_gen.SLOTS`.
4. `make fpga-twin` (from the repo root); a test image that reads it goes in
   `asm/` and in `fpga_twin_suite.CARD_IMAGES`.

A drawn card is the same steps with `kind: 'kicad'` and `sch` in place
of `src`: draw it with a `DINO_BUS_EDGE`, give its off-edge parts
`io_in`/`io_out` (ref, pin, port) and bind those ports with `bind`/`outputs`.
`window` is then what its own '138 decodes, stated so the oracle and the
slot table's two-cards-one-window check know it. The usual path is RTL
first, then the drawing, keeping the oracle and the test image.

`docs/notes/test_fpga_slots.py` holds every card to the API (exact ports,
synthesizes alone; each KiCad card's edge against its netlist; RTL cards
drive W only inside their window on a read) and shows the recipe working
end to end.

## The serial line to the host

The serial line the Mac sees runs through the serial card in J2, as on the
board; nothing bypasses it:

    host <-> Versa B19 (uart_tx) / B12 (uart_rx), 9600 8N1        versa.lpf
    versa_top.v   uart_rx -> two-flop sync -> sin;  sout -> uart_tx
    dino_system   sin -> j2.io_SIN_xo;  sout = j2.io_SOUT
    dino_serial   io_SIN -> U103.10 (SIN);  U103.11 (SOUT) -> /RXD -> io_SOUT
                  (/RXD is named from the FTDI side: J1 pin 4)

The CPU reaches U103 only through the edge: `~{IO_SEL}` and M11-M13 into
U101, `~{SER_SEL}` to U103.14, W through U102 onto SD0-SD7, `~{IO_WR}`,
`~{IO_RD_Q}`, M0-M2, `RESET_B`. U103 is `f_16550` in `ttl_fpga.v`, a
behavioural UART; its XIN is a 3.6864 MHz phase accumulator on `clk_sys`, so
the bit rate on the wire is right. The chips around it are the drawn ones.
Logic, not timing: the breadboard's received-bit loss (`SERIAL_RX_A0`, the
22 pF on `U103.28`) cannot appear here.

In Verilator, `sim/twin_tb.cpp` is the host: `+SIN=<text>` sends 8N1 into
`sin` (`\r`, `\n`; `~` = two seconds idle; `+GAP_BITS`, `+SIN2`/`+SIN2_AT`
for a second burst) and it decodes `sout`. `fpga_twin_suite.py --cards` runs
the serial images that way.

## Commands

    make gen                     regenerate gen/ for the slot table
    make bit IMAGE=deadbeef      build/dino_deadbeef.bit, roms/PROG_deadbeef.bin in U24
    make prog IMAGE=deadbeef     load it into the Versa (SRAM)
    make -C .. fpga-twin         every image, 3 power-up seeds, against the oracle

Versa mapping (`versa_top.v`, `versa.lpf`): LEDs read OB like a byte (bit 7 on
D25), DIPs are card zero's SW1 (bit 7 on H2, ON = closed), serial TX B19 /
RX B12 at 9600 8N1, reset button T1.

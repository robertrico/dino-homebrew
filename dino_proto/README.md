# dino_proto — the DINO prototype slot card

A perfboard with the bus edge and the slot decode every DINO card
repeats. Plug it into any of J1-J8 on the Rev 1.0 mainboard, pick the
slot with one shunt, bodge the card on the grid. Generated by
`docs/notes/proto_card_gen.py` from `bus_gen.BUS`; proved by
`docs/notes/test_proto_card.py`. Design and history: `.git/sdd/PROTO_CARD.md`.

    python3 docs/notes/proto_card_gen.py --write      # the project, unrouted
    python3 docs/notes/proto_card_gen.py --route 200  # Freerouting the band, DRC after

191.8 x 70 mm, 2-layer, all through-hole. The finger tab (65.8 mm) is
near the right end: the body runs 18 mm past the tab's right end (the
mainboard's edge is a future case wall; the cap is 20) and 108 mm to the
left, over the mainboard.

## What is on it

| Ref | Part | Does |
|-----|------|------|
| J1  | 2x25 fingers | the bus, pin n opposite pin n+25 (`PERIPHERAL_BUS.md` section 1) |
| J2  | 2x25 pin header | the edge repeated 1:1, pin n = edge pin n, numbered BY ROWS (1-25 front row, nearest the fingers; 26-50 back row) for a 50-way IDC ribbon to a breadboard. Silk: one label per column, "front/back", read as a picture of the column: the word at the top of the label is the top (back) hole, the word at the bottom the bottom (front) hole; "F/B" at pin 1; SPnn = spare, 3V3 = +3.3V |
| U1  | 74LS138 | slot decode: A0-A2 = M11-M13, E1 = `~{IO_SEL}`, E2 = GND, E3 = +5V, as the serial card's U101 |
| JP1 | 2x8 header | slot select, ONE shunt |
| U2  | 74LS32 | `~{CARD_RD}` = `~{CARD_SEL}` or `~{IO_RD_Q}`; `~{CARD_WR}` = `~{CARD_SEL}` or `~{IO_WR}`; two gates spare |
| J3  | 2x6 header | the decoded signals for the bodge |
| J4  | 1x4 header | the spare '32 gates |
| R1  | 4.7k | `~{CARD_SEL}` pull-up (no shunt = no window) |
| R2, R3 | 4.7k | park the spare gates (A high); leave one out to use its gate, see J4 |
| C1, C2 | 100 nF | U1, U2 decoupling |
| C3  | 10 uF | +5V bulk at the edge |

## JP1 — slot select

Top row `O0`..`O7` (the '138's outputs, left to right, silk 0-7); bottom
row all `~{CARD_SEL}`. One shunt in column n puts the card in slot n:

| slot | window        | slot | window        |
|------|---------------|------|---------------|
| 0    | 0x4000-0x47FF | 4    | 0x6000-0x67FF |
| 1    | 0x4800-0x4FFF | 5    | 0x6800-0x6FFF |
| 2    | 0x5000-0x57FF | 6    | 0x7000-0x77FF |
| 3    | 0x5800-0x5FFF | 7    | 0x7800-0x7FFF |

Two shunts short two '138 outputs together: never. Slot 0 is card zero
(the DIP switch), slot 1 the serial card, on the breadboard machine.

## J3 — the card's signals

| pin | signal          | pin | signal |
|-----|-----------------|-----|--------|
| 1   | `~{CARD_SEL}`   | 7   | `O3`   |
| 2   | `~{CARD_RD}`    | 8   | `O4`   |
| 3   | `~{CARD_WR}`    | 9   | `O5`   |
| 4   | `O0`            | 10  | `O6`   |
| 5   | `O1`            | 11  | `O7`   |
| 6   | `O2`            | 12  | GND    |

`O0`-`O7` are the raw decoder outputs, for a bodge that wants a second
window. The rule for every card (PHASE_G): drive W only while
`~{CARD_RD}` is low, take data on `~{CARD_WR}`; nothing else goes back
on the bus (CARD-2).

## J4 — the spare '32 gates

| pin | signal | gate |
|-----|--------|------|
| 1   | 3B     | gate 3 input; 3A pulled up by R2 |
| 2   | 3Y     | gate 3 output |
| 3   | 4B     | gate 4 input; 4A pulled up by R3 |
| 4   | 4Y     | gate 4 output |

With A pulled high an OR gate's output is high whatever B does. To use
a spare gate as a 2-input OR, leave its pull-up out (R2 for gate 3, R3
for gate 4) and wire A at the empty resistor's pad: the one on the U2
side is U2 pin 9 (3A) / pin 12 (4A). With the pull-up fitted the gate
is parked.

## The grid

0.1 in plated holes, drill 1.0 mm, pad 1.8 mm, both sides, every hole
on its own. Rows top to bottom: +12, -12, 3V3 (square holes, one row
each), then +5V / A-E / GND, +5V / F-J / GND, +5V / K-M. A rail row is a
row of SQUARE holes joined by a masked trace (GND 1.5 mm, +5V 1.2 mm,
bias 0.6 mm, both layers); the round holes beside it are free. A 0.3 in
DIP sits on a band's rows 1 and 4 with VCC and GND one row away.
Columns 1-71 numbered every fifth on the top edge, rows lettered on the
right. No track of the card's own copper runs through the grid (rule
areas, DRC-checked).

The band's own +5V and GND rails are hole-less traces just above the
chips, fed from the grid's rows down the left margin. The six J2-to-band
signals (M11-M13, `~{IO_SEL}`, `~{IO_RD_Q}`, `~{IO_WR}`) run as lanes
between the grid and the band.

## Rails on the edge

+5V, GND, +12V, -12V, +3.3V are what the ATX unit delivers (POWER.md
section 8). -12V is 50-100 mA for the whole machine. This card draws
nothing from +12V/-12V/+3.3V itself; a bodge that does declares it.

## Fab

PCBWay 2-layer, 1.6 mm, gold fingers with a bevel. Open before the
first order: the Sullins EBC25DCWN slot opening against the 65.8 mm tab
(no catalog on disk; `PERIPHERAL_BUS.md` OPEN 2).
